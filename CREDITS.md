## AI-generated images (round-two previews)

Every image under `previews/supper/img/`, `previews/seance/img/` and `previews/scrim/img/` was
generated for Vesper with an AI image-generation model (Factory's `GenerateImage` tool) in
October 2026. No photographs of real people or existing artworks were used as inputs. The
characters are fictional. Source PNGs were resized and converted to WebP with ffmpeg.

- **Supper base portraits** (`previews/supper/img/<name>-neutral.webp`, nine characters): text
  prompts for a Baroque chiaroscuro character portrait (head and shoulders, single candle light
  from below, dark background, 17th-century clothing), one per character description.
- **Expression variants** (`-glanceL`, `-glanceR`, `-afraid`, `-speak`, `-closed`): image edits of
  each base portrait asking to change only the eyes, mouth or expression (sidelong glance left or
  right, frightened with beads of sweat, mid-word, eyes closed) and keep identity, pose, lighting
  and background the same.
- **Supper props** (`prop-letter`, `prop-snuff`, `prop-hands`, `prop-table`): text prompts for
  Baroque still-life photographs of a sealed letter, a hand pinching out a candle, two hands
  passing a note, and an empty candlelit table.
- **Séance tintypes** (`previews/seance/img/`): image edits of the Supper base portraits restyled
  as antique sepia tintype plates, then expression edits (glances, fear and sweat; eyes closed and
  speaking for two characters).
- **Scrim close-ups** (`previews/scrim/img/<name>-*.webp`): image edits of the Supper base
  portraits restyled as high-contrast black-and-white silent-film close-ups, then expression edits
  (anguish with tears, speaking, eyes closed).
- **Scrim shadow bodies** (`previews/scrim/img/pose-*.webp`): text prompts for solid black
  full-body silhouettes of human poses on white (standing, pointing, recoiling, whispering,
  kneeling, falling, voting, grieving, dancing, carrying a lantern, lying down), converted to
  transparent cut-outs.

- **Séance table and objects** (`previews/seance/img/table.webp`, `hand.webp`, `candle-lit.webp`,
  `candle-out.webp`): text prompts for a top-down round Victorian séance table in dark oak with an
  oxblood velvet cloth (then an edit removing the centre candle dish), a pair of translucent
  glowing spirit hands on black (one hand cropped and keyed to transparency), and a lit white
  candle in a brass holder seen from above (then an edit of the same frame just blown out, with
  smoke).

## AI-generated images (the game, `src/ui/assets/`)

Generated the same way and under the same terms as the round-two preview images above (Factory's
`GenerateImage` tool, October 2026; fictional characters; no photographs of real people or
existing artworks as inputs), then resized and converted to WebP with ffmpeg.

- **Faces** (`src/ui/assets/faces/`): the nine Scrim close-ups above, plus six new characters
  (`f10`–`f15`) from text prompts for high-contrast black-and-white silent-film close-ups, each
  followed by expression edits (afraid, speaking, eyes closed) that keep identity, framing and
  lighting the same.
- **Shadow bodies** (`src/ui/assets/poses/`): the twelve Scrim silhouettes above, plus twelve new
  text prompts for solid black full-body silhouettes on white (aiming a pistol, a deep bow, carrying
  a candle, hooded in a cloak, creeping, guarding a doorway, tending a patient, knocking, lunging,
  sitting, struggling, walking), converted to transparent cut-outs.
- **The Mourner's hand** (`src/ui/assets/hand/`): two text prompts for a solid black hand
  silhouette on white, one pressed flat with the palm forward and one reaching with the fingers
  curled, converted to transparent cut-outs.
