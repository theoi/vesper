Vesper

Three art & sound directions for a social deduction game. Each is a short live sequence with an original procedural score: title, role, nightfall, dawn, the square, the trial and an execution. Pick the one you want the whole game to wear.

Direction I

Masque

surrealist opera

The town is an opera staged inside a dream: porcelain masks stand on plinths in a metaphysical piazza where the sun never quite sets. Every accusation is an aria and every death a curtain call, scored for a synthetic choir, bells, harp and timpani.

Direction II

Agitprop

constructivist broadside

The town is a constructivist poster that reprints itself every day: votes are red wedges and verdicts are rubber stamps. The score is a factory of anvils, telegraph keys and brass, and every movement lands on the beat.

Direction III

Dead Air

brutalist glitch broadcast

The town is a pirate broadcast going off the air: every player is a channel on a wall of monitors and every lie is interference. When you die your signal is cut, and a modular synth hisses where you used to be.
