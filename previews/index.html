Vesper
Round two · three human directions

Faces that glance, sweat and flinch when they are accused; a gathering around one table; bodies acting out the town. Each direction is scored with recorded human voices and acoustic instruments mixed with live synthesis, and plays the same short sequence: title, role, nightfall, dawn, the day, the trial and an execution. Pick the one you want the whole game to wear.

Direction I

Supper

chiaroscuro portraits at one long table

Nine faces painted like a Baroque supper, lit only by the candles in front of them. They glance at whoever speaks, sweat when they are named and go dark one candle at a time. Scored for piano, strings and the murmur of a real room.

Direction II

Séance

living tintypes around a round table

Nine tintype photographs laid around a séance table, breathing under one candle. Spectral hands knock out the votes, whispers circle the room and a single breath puts out the light.

Direction III

Scrim

shadow theatre & silent close-ups

Bodies behind a lit cloth act out the town, leaning in, pointing and falling, cut against silent-film close-ups of faces in tears. Scored for a cappella voices and body percussion.
